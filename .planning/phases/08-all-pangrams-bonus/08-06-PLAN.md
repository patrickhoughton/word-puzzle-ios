  <what-built>Completion bonuses (Plans 08-01..08-05): pangram sweep (+7 per pangram) with a tally card, length-completion bonus (+L) with a pill, a board pangram counter, a Found Words pangram line and "+L" on complete groups, an overflow glow past 100%, the hidden "Mythic Grandmaster" tier, round-end bonus lines, and three new Kenney clips (sweep fanfare = maximize_008, tick = tick_002, length chime = confirmation_002).   <how-to-verify>
    Ring/silent switch OFF (sound on), Settings > Sound Effects on. Tip: the longest word lengths usually have only 1 to 3 words, so they are the quickest way to trigger a length bonus.
    1. Board: under the rank row, left side shows a seal icon and "0/N" (N = pangrams in this puzzle). Free users still see "X of 3 free puzzles today" on the right of that same row.
    2. Find a word that is the only word of its length (or finish a short group). Expect about 1/4 s after the normal "+points" feedback: a pill over the top of the letter grid, "<L> Letters complete! +<L>" with "+<L>" in gold, a light chime (not the usual accept sound), and a success tap. It fades after about 1.5 s. You can keep typing during it.
    3. Find every pangram (open Found Words to see "Pangrams · X of N"; the board counter goes 1/N, 2/N...). On the last one: a card counts +7, +14 ... with each pangram word flashing and a tick per step, then "Pangram sweep! +N" in gold with a fanfare (clearly different from the single-pangram sound) and a strong success tap. Whole thing is under about 2.5 s. The board counter icon turns into a gold filled seal.
    4. If that last pangram was also the only word of its length: the pill shows first, then the tally card; they never overlap.
    5. Keep going until score passes the puzzle max (may need many words; skip if impractical): the full bar shows a gold glow and a moving shimmer, and the rank reads "Mythic Grandmaster" in gold with sparkles. At exactly full (Legend) there is no shimmer.
    6. Open Found Words: completed groups show the gold check plus a grey "+L"; the header line reads "Pangrams · X of N".
    7. Trigger a celebration and immediately tap Finish Round: the overlay disappears instantly; the missed-words screen shows "Pangram sweep! +N" (gold) if earned and "Length bonuses +M" if earned. Tap Next Puzzle: board counter is back to "0/N", no leftover overlay.
    8. Settings > Accessibility > Motion > Reduce Motion ON: the pill and sweep card simply fade (no slide/scale, no counting, no ticks); the fanfare and haptic still play; the bar shows a static glow with no shimmer.
    9. Flip the silent switch ON (or turn Sound Effects off in the app): celebrations and haptics still happen, with no sound.
    10. VoiceOver ON: completing a length says e.g. "5 letters complete, plus 5 points"; the sweep says "Pangram sweep! plus N points" once (no intermediate counts). The score bar reads "... 1 of 3 pangrams found." (and "Progress beyond maximum." when overflowing).
    11. Larger Text at the largest accessibility size: the detail row (counter + free puzzles), the pill, the tally card and (if reached) "Mythic Grandmaster" stay on one line (may shrink), and the control row is not pushed off screen.
    12. Sound choices: do the fanfare, tick and chime feel right? If not, name which to swap (other candidates in the Kenney pack: maximize_009, confirmation_003, tick_001, tick_004).
