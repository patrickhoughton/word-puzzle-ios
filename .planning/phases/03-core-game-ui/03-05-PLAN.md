  <how-to-verify>
Launch the app on a real iPhone and work through these eight checks in order. Report PASS/FAIL for each.

1. **Launch and grid layout (GAME-01, D-01)**
   The app opens directly into the game screen — no debug panel, no "Hello, world!". After a brief "Loading words..." it shows 7 hexagonal tiles arranged as a honeycomb flower: 1 in the center, 6 around it. The center tile is filled honeycomb gold (#F5B800) and is clearly distinguished from the 6 grey outer tiles.

2. **Tap-to-append (GAME-01, D-02)**
   Tap 4 different letters one at a time, making sure to include the gold center letter. Each tap appends exactly one letter to the word shown above the grid, in tap order. No taps are dropped, no letter appears twice from a single tap.

3. **Drag-to-connect (GAME-01, D-02)**
   Without lifting your finger, drag continuously across 4–5 tiles. Each tile the finger crosses appends its letter, in crossing order. Then immediately do check 2 again (tap-only) on the same round — confirm tapping still works after dragging. This alternation is the specific failure mode RESEARCH Pitfall 1 warns about.

4. **Swipe-down submit + valid-word feedback (GAME-01, GAME-02, GAME-03, RET-03, D-06/D-08)**
   Build a real word (4+ letters, must include the center letter). Drag the assembled word downward ~60pt and release. It submits. Confirm ALL of:
   - You physically FEEL a haptic tap (RET-03)
   - A gold "+N" appears briefly and the word display pops
   - The rank tier name and the "N of M words" count both update
   Then find and submit a SECOND valid word and confirm the haptic fires again — if the second one is silent, that is RESEARCH Pitfall 3 and it is a FAIL.

5. **Invalid word rejection (GAME-02, D-07)**
   Build a nonsense letter sequence and swipe down to submit. The word display shakes, "Not a valid word" appears in red, and the app does NOT crash. Then submit the same valid word from check 4 a second time — it should also be rejected (duplicate).

6. **Delete and clear (D-05)**
   Add 3 letters, tap the Delete (`delete.left`) button 3 times — one letter is removed per tap. Add 3 more letters and tap directly on the assembled word — it clears entirely.

7. **Shuffle animation (PUZZ-04, D-03)**
   Tap the Shuffle (`shuffle`) button several times. The 6 outer letters ANIMATE into new positions — they must not instantly jump. The gold center letter never moves. Immediately after a shuffle, drag across tiles and confirm the correct letters are appended (not stale pre-shuffle letters).

8. **Finish Round and missed-words reveal (GAME-04, D-10/D-11/D-12)**
   Tap "Finish Round". A full-screen "Words You Missed" list appears, grouped under "4 Letters", "5 Letters", etc. headers, and any missed pangram carries a gold "Pangram" badge. Tap "Next Puzzle" — a brand new puzzle with different letters appears immediately, with the score reset and the rank back to "Novice".
  </how-to-verify>
