<objective>
Wire the Plan 01 logic into the game screen: double-tap on empty space shuffles the 6 outer letters, alongside the unchanged Shuffle button, with a light haptic on every real shuffle.

Two empty regions, two owners, and no new recognizer on the tiles:
- Inside the honeycomb's square: the grid's existing single `DragGesture(minimumDistance: 0)` feeds `EmptyDoubleTapDetector` and calls a new `onEmptyDoubleTap` callback (D-02).
- Everywhere else: a `Color.clear` background layer behind `playingLayout` with `onTapGesture(count: 2)` (D-03). It sits BEHIND every child, so it only gets touches no child claims. Buttons, the word display (it has `contentShape` + `onTapGesture`), and the grid square never reach it (D-04, D-05). The research doc offered an ancestor gesture on the VStack as its primary option and this background layer as the fallback. We use the background layer because it puts no gesture above the grid or the word display, so there is no parent-vs-child priority question and no risk to tile tap latency (Phase 3 Pitfall 1).

Purpose: This is the user-facing feature of Phase 10.
Output: Modified LetterGridView.swift and GameView.swift.
</objective>
