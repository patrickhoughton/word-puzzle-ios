  <name>Task 2: On-device gesture, haptic, and latency check</name>
  <what-built>Double-tap empty space to shuffle, both inside the honeycomb's gaps and on the background around it, plus a light haptic on every real shuffle (Shuffle button and gesture). The Debug build is installed on your iPhone.</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Start a round on the phone, then:
    1. Double-tap an empty margin (left or right of the honeycomb), the space between the word display and the honeycomb, and the space below the honeycomb. Each should shuffle the 6 outer letters with one light tick.
    2. Double-tap in a gap between two hexagons and in a corner of the honeycomb's square. Each should shuffle.
    3. Quickly tap one tile twice. The letter should be added twice with no shuffle. Single tile taps and drag-across-tiles should feel as instant as before.
    4. Tap a tile, then quickly tap a gap. Nothing should shuffle.
    5. Double-tap the word display (it should clear and not shuffle), then the Shuffle, Delete, Finish Round, stats, settings, and score-bar buttons. None of them should shuffle from a double-tap. Each should just do its own action.
    6. Tap the Shuffle button. It should shuffle with the same light tick. Double-tap the background several times very fast. Letters should shuffle at most once per animation, with one tick per real shuffle.
    7. Type a few letters, then double-tap the background. The letters should shuffle and the typed word should stay.
    8. Open Stats or Settings and double-tap inside the sheet. Nothing behind it should shuffle. Finish the round and double-tap the round-over screen. Nothing should shuffle.
    If a double-tap feels too strict or too loose (timing or distance between taps), say so. The 0.3s and 44pt thresholds in EmptyDoubleTapDetector.swift can be tuned.
  </how-to-verify>
