Word Puzzle Unlimited Support

Questions, bug reports, or a word you think should count? Email phoughton@threemilebridge.com.

How to play

  • Tap or drag across the letters to build a word, then swipe down on the word to submit it.
  • Words must be at least four letters long and include the gold center letter. Letters can be reused.
  • A pangram uses all seven letters and earns a bonus.
  • Tap Finish Round to end the round and see the words you missed.

Free puzzles and unlimited play

You can play 3 free puzzles per day; the count resets at midnight. A one-time purchase of “Unlimited Puzzles” removes the limit. It is not a subscription.

Restoring your purchase

On a new or reset device, open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
